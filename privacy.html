Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

GRINLOUD ("we", "us") takes your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how we handle it.

1. What we collect

The only personal data we collect is your email address when you subscribe to our newsletter. We do not use cookies, analytics trackers, or any third-party tracking on this website.

2. Why we collect it

Your email is used solely to send you the Pick of the Day and Music Radar newsletter. Nothing else.

3. Who processes your data

We use the following services to manage and deliver email:

  • Beehiiv (newsletter platform) — beehiiv.com/privacy
  • Resend (email delivery) — resend.com/privacy

Both services are GDPR-compliant. Your data is stored on servers in the EU/USA in accordance with applicable data protection law.

4. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your data at any time. You can unsubscribe from the newsletter via the link in any email we send. For other requests, contact us at hello@grinloud.com.

5. Data retention

We retain your email address as long as you are subscribed. Upon unsubscribing, your data is removed from our active list. Deletion requests are processed within 30 days.

6. Contact

For privacy-related questions: hello@grinloud.com
