Privacy Policy

We take your privacy seriously. Short version: we only collect your email address, we use it only to send you music, and you can leave any time.

WHAT WE COLLECT

The only personal data we collect is your email address when you subscribe to our newsletter. We do not use cookies or behavioral trackers. WHY WE COLLECT IT

Your email is used solely to send you the Pick of the Day and Music Radar. Nothing else. We will never sell, share, or misuse your data.

DATA PROCESSORS

Both services are GDPR-compliant. Data is processed in accordance with applicable Swiss and EU data protection law (DSG / GDPR).

YOUR RIGHTS

You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in any email we send. For access, correction, or deletion requests: hello@grinloud.com

RETENTION

We retain your email address as long as you are subscribed. Deletion requests are processed within 30 days.
