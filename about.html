House Music Curated Daily

WHAT IT IS

GRINLOUD is a daily curated house music platform. Every day, one Pick of the Day —
a single hand-picked track worth your attention. Every 10 days, Music Radar — a
10-track playlist of the best new releases across the genres we cover.

GENRES

House · Tech House · Progressive House · Bass House

THE RHYTHM

Pick of the Day — every day.
Music Radar — every 10 days, 10 tracks.
No feed to scroll, no algorithm — just what's worth hearing right now.

WHO IT'S FOR

DJs digging for the next track, radio shows looking for fresh selections, and
anyone who wants a fast, reliable read on new house music without wading through
everything else.

WHO'S BEHIND IT

GRINLOUD is curated by Cy Schneider, run by Isuntu GmbH.
Every pick and every Radar selection passes through one ear before it's published.
