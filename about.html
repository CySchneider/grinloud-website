Hi — I'm Grin. Yellow head, permanent smile, professional House Music enthusiast. This page is me explaining who we are and why we bother doing this every single day.

WHAT IT IS

GRINLOUD is a daily curated house music platform — new Tech House, Bass House, Progressive House and House tracks, hand-picked every single day. Every day, one Pick of the Day — a single track worth your attention. Every 10 days, the GRINLOUD Music Radar — a Tech House and Bass House mix of the 10 best new releases, all mixed into one continuous set you can listen to start to finish.

No robot picked any of it. Someone actually listened.

WHO WE ARE

Two subjects. One human, one digital.

Cy is the human: curator, DJ, house music lover. He listens until the transition sits right, until nothing repeats, until the set makes sense start to finish.

Grin, that's me, is the digital one: energizer, story-dancer, a yellow head that never stops grinning. Built on purpose — a symbol for what house music feels like: energy, joy, a grin that doesn't fade.

One Pick every day. One Radar every 10 days. No pause, for months — because love for music doesn't take breaks, and it doesn't need to. It's what runs our life.

GRINLOUD is curated by Cy Schneider. Every pick and every Radar selection passes through one ear before it's published.

WHY WE DO THIS

No investors. No sponsors picking the tracks. No secret plan to turn a grin into a cash machine.

WHO IT'S FOR

House music lovers who want a daily dose of new tracks to get inspired by, and a mix of the 10 best ones to lose themselves in every 10 days. Also for DJs digging for the next track, and radio shows looking for fresh selections.
